House Appraiser

Playable homes: 4,407.
Includes listings across 24 cities in 14 states. Players choose a city before
playing.

To run:
1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run: python3 -m http.server 8080
3. Open: http://localhost:8080

The game intentionally shows city/state and home facts before the guess,
while keeping the listing price hidden until the user submits a guess.
